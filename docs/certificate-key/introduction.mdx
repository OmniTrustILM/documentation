# Introduction

Cryptographic keys and digital certificates are the foundation of security in our digital world. They are used to secure communication, authentication, and digital signatures. The platform ILM is designed to help you manage and automate your cryptographic keys and digital certificates in a secure and efficient way.

The core of the platform is released under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/OmniTrustILM/ilm/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Connectors and providers can be developed against the published OpenAPI specifications by anyone.
